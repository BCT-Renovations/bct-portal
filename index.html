About BCT Renovations

We’re Not Just Connecting You With a Contractor. We Are Your General Contractor.

At BCT Renovations, LLC, homeowners deserve more than just a name and a phone number. You deserve to know who is working on your property, that they have been properly verified, and that someone is standing with you throughout the entire project.

That’s where BCT comes in.

Contractors in our network go through the BCT verification process, including verification of required credentials and current insurance documentation before they are approved to perform work through our platform.

BCT doesn’t simply make the connection and walk away. We help coordinate your project, assign qualified contractors, monitor progress, and remain your point of contact from the beginning of the project through completion.

Our goal is to take the guesswork out of hiring someone to work on your home. You shouldn’t have to wonder who to trust, whether the proper documentation is current, or who to turn to if something isn’t going the way it should.

You don’t have to fight that battle alone. BCT stands with the homeowner.

We bring the contractors. We oversee the process. We help protect the quality of the project. We give you one company to turn to from start to finish.
